About the shop

A barber's chair, not a salon.

Allan's sits on East Laith Gate, just off the market square — a small independent gents barber doing the cuts people in Doncaster have been asking for for years. Short back & sides, fades, beards tidied up, the occasional classic shave.

Walk in. Sit down. Tell us what you want. Go about your day.

What we do.

Prices on the board inside the shop. Cash & card both fine.

01 / Cuts

Haircut

Short back and sides, scissor cuts, skin fades, crops. Whatever you've been asking for — show us a picture if it's easier.

02 / Faces

Beard Trim

Shaped, lined, tidied. Cleaned up around the cheekline and neck. Good for keeping a beard looking like it's meant to be there.

03 / Traditional

Wet Shave

Hot towel, soap and a straight razor. Ten minutes of being looked after properly. Ask about availability on the day.

04 / In between

Tidy Up

A quick neck-and-edge trim if you're keeping the same shape but it's grown out. In and out — no big sit-down.
